How to use

  1. Load a vocal: open a file, record, or try the demo.
  2. Each sung note appears as an orange block. The red line is the pitch that was sung.
  3. Drag a note up or down to change its pitch. It snaps to the scale; hold Alt to go in between.
  4. Turn up Center to pull every note to the nearest scale note at once. Variation removes vibrato and wobble. Transition sets how fast pitch moves between notes.
  5. Press Space to listen. Original lets you compare with the untouched audio.
  6. Export WAV when you're happy.

Fixing wrong notes

  • ✂ Cut (C), then click a note to split it.
  • Merge (G) joins the selected note with its neighbour.
  • Delete removes a note, leaving that part untouched.
  • 〰 Line (P) lets you bend the pitch line itself, including the glides between notes. Drag up or down on the line; Width sets how much of it moves, Shift makes it a quarter as sensitive for fine adjustment, and a double-click straightens that part again.
