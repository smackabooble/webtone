Help

Getting started

  1. Load a vocal from File, record with the microphone button, or try the demo.
  2. Each sung note is an orange block. The line is the pitch that was sung.
  3. Drag a note up or down to change its pitch. It snaps to the key and scale; hold Alt to go in between.
  4. The sliders on the right work on every note. Center pulls notes to the scale, Variation removes vibrato and wobble, Transition sets how fast pitch moves between notes.
  5. Space plays. Original compares with the untouched audio. Export when you're happy.

Tools

  • Select V moves notes. Use Selected note on the right for an exact offset.
  • Cut C splits a note where you click. Choose what cuts snap to.
  • Pitch line P bends the line itself, including glides between notes. Shift for fine, double-click to straighten.
  • Merge G joins the selected note with its neighbour. Delete removes it.

Tempo

The BPM box is the tempo you hear. Say what tempo the recording was made at under Recorded at, then change BPM to speed the audio up or slow it down by up to 2×, without changing its pitch. The ruler's bars and beats follow it, and cuts can snap to the beat.
